How to get here

Woods Square Solo 1, #04-43

  1. 01

    Start at Woodlands MRT

    Exit Woodlands MRT (NS9 / TE2) towards Causeway Point and Woods Square. Choose your route:

    • Sheltered 4 to 5 min, under cover all the way
    • Direct 3 to 4 min, partly open to the sky
  2. 02

    Find the Tower 1 | Solo 1 sign

    Follow the covered walkway to the Woods Square sign marked Tower 1 and Solo 1. Solo 1 is the low block beside Tower 1.

  3. 03

    Take the lift to level 4

    Enter the Solo 1 lift lobby and go up to level 4. We are at unit #04-43.

  1. 01

    Drive to 10 Woodlands Square

    Set your GPS to Woods Square Solo 1. There are two ways into the Basement 2 carpark:

    • Option 1 Solo 1 Basement 2 carpark entrance
    • Option 2 Enter from the Tower 2 carpark entrance
  2. 02

    Park anywhere at Basement 2

    The Basement 2 carparks of Tower 1, Tower 2, Solo 1 and Solo 2 are all linked, so either entrance works. Follow the signs to the Solo 1 lifts.

  3. 03

    Take the lift to level 4

    From the Solo 1 lift lobby, go up to level 4. We are at unit #04-43.
